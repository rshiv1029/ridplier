PA JOB HELPER: how to use

1. Double-click "start_mac.command". (First time only: right-click it, choose Open, then Open again.
   Setup takes a few minutes. Later it starts in seconds.)
2. A page opens in your browser. Click "My info", fill in your details, upload your resume, Save.
3. On the Jobs page, newest jobs are at the top. Click "Apply" on one.
4. A new browser window opens. Log in or create the hospital account the first time.
   The app fills in what it safely can. Questions about licenses, DEA, or anything
   needing your own answer are outlined in ORANGE. Answer those yourself
   (choose "pending" for licenses that are still in process).
5. Click Next in that window yourself. The app fills the next page automatically.
6. Click Submit yourself, then press "I applied" on the job card.

Check the page each morning. It looks for new jobs every time you open it.
To quit, close the black Terminal window.
